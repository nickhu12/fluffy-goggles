#python-portfolio

Multiplication Quiz - This program/computer generates any 2 numbers to quiz you. You have your statistics at the end based on your correct and incorrect answers. Having 5 questions to quiz you and by doing this, it can strengthen your simple multiplication skills.

Pokemon - This code you play pokemon like normal but coding format. You start off with a starter pokemon. Play your way to evolve your pokemon to stage 3. By battling or training.

RPS - This code allows you to play Rock Paper Scissors with the computer. You pick Rock(1), Paper(2), or Scissors(3) and the computer will randomize one and see if you win,lose, or even tie. At the end of your games you will have your score (Wins, Loses, and Ties).

Simple Calc - Calculates your 2 numbers in 4 ways. Select a way to calculate your numbers. Then input your numbers and the computer/code will automatically calculate it for you.
              Mutliplication, Division, Addition, Subtraction

Ticket Generator - This code generates your ticket price depending on your age and day of week. If you have a coupon it will also make your ticket cheaper. Depending on your age and day of week your ticket can range from 0-100$.
